Claudeslop is a place to discover and share little games made with AI. Strange experiments and rough edges are welcome. Give people something fun to play.

The house rules

  • Share a playable browser game. No installers, malware, gambling, or payment required to try it.
  • Give credit. Tell us who made it and how AI was involved. Don't repost other people's work as your own.
  • Use a real gameplay screenshot. Keep titles and descriptions accurate.
  • No hate, harassment, explicit sexual content, or spam. We can remove submissions that don't belong.
  • Vote for games you enjoyed. Votes are saved in this browser; clearing cookies resets your voting identity.

A small prototype

Submissions go live immediately. We store your submission, an anonymous browser cookie for votes, and temporary hashed network identifiers for spam limits. We don't host submitted games. Play opens the creator's site in a new tab; its own policies apply. External preview images may contact their host.

To report a game, open a report on GitHub ↗ with its title and link.
